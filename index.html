Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to buy both marketing and CRM services?+

No — most clients start with one and add the other once they see the gap it closes. That said, the biggest wins come from running both together, since it's the handoff between them where leads usually get lost.

Which CRM platforms do you support?+

We're partnered with Salesforce, Microsoft (Dynamics & Power Platform) and Zoho, and we'll recommend whichever fits your size, budget and existing tech stack — not just the one we're pushing.

How long until we see results?+

Most implementations go live in weeks, not months. Marketing results build over the first 60–90 days as campaigns and content gain traction; CRM improvements — like leads no longer falling through the cracks — are usually visible from week one.

What does it cost?+

It depends on scope — a lean CRM setup for a 10-person team looks very different from a full Salesforce COE plus paid media program. The free discovery call ends with a clear, itemised quote, no obligation attached.

We're a startup with almost no marketing or CRM in place — is that a problem?+

That's actually the easiest starting point — no legacy system to untangle. Boltin's Startups track is built exactly for this stage.
